- **Subtitle (iOS, 30 chars):** Baby tracker & parent care

- **Category:** Parenting (or Health & Fitness if Parenting is unavailable)

- **Keywords (iOS):** baby tracker, feeding log, sleep tracker, diaper, newborn, twins, postpartum, pumping, parenting

## Full description
My Bean is a calm, simple way to keep track of your baby's day, and a place to look after yourself too.

**Track in one tap**
Log feedings and diapers in a tap, start a sleep timer, and undo mistakes right away. Expecting? Add your due date and see how many days to go.

**Twins, triplets and siblings**
Give every baby their own profile and logs, or log for all of them at once.

**Share with your partner**
Link two parents with an invite code. You both see the same logs and who logged what.

**See the patterns**
Charts for sleep, feedings and diapers over 7 or 30 days, plus a summary you can share with your doctor.

**Gentle reminders**
Optional reminders for the next feeding, nap windows, and a weekly check-in for you.

**Your wellbeing matters**
Private daily check-ins and the Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale screening, with support resources. Only you can see them.

**Community**
Ask questions and share stories with other parents. Report or block anyone, with moderators keeping the space kind.

*My Bean provides general information and tracking tools. It is not medical advice and the wellbeing screening is not a diagnosis. Always talk to a health professional about medical concerns.*

## Apple "App Privacy" answers
Data **collected and linked to the user**, used only for **App Functionality**, **not used for tracking**:
- Contact Info: Name, Email Address
- Health & Fitness: Health (wellbeing check-ins, baby health logs)
- User Content: Photos (profile photo), Other User Content (posts, notes)
- Identifiers: User ID

No third-party advertising, no analytics SDKs, no data sold. Encryption in transit: yes. Account deletion: in-app (Settings -> Delete my account).

## Google Play "Data safety" answers
- **Data collected:** Personal info (name, email address, user IDs), Health info (wellbeing and baby health logs), Photos (profile photo), Messages/other user-generated content (community posts), App activity (logs the user enters).
- **Shared with third parties:** none. (Our hosting provider processes data on our behalf; that is not "sharing".)
- **Purpose:** App functionality, Account management.
- **Encrypted in transit:** yes.
- **Users can request deletion:** yes: in-app, plus the web link you publish (see RELEASE.md step 1).
- **Optional vs required:** profile photo, wellbeing and posts are optional; email and name are required.
